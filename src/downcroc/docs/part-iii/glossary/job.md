---
term: job
see_also: [queued, sent, failed, batch]
---

One email that downcroc has been asked to send, together with everything it
needs: the recipient, the subject, the body, and the record of what happened
when it tried. A job is the unit you create, watch, and cancel. It exists
before it is sent and after, so the history is always readable.

More: [jobs](../part-ii/14d-jobs.md)

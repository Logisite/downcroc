# Threading a reply

When you follow up, you want your note to land in the same conversation as the
one before it. A reply that shows up as a brand new thread feels like you
forgot you had already written; a reply in the right place feels like you are
continuing the thought. downcroc keeps follow-ups in the same [[thread]] on
purpose.

## One conversation, step by step

A follow-up is sent as a reply to the parent message for that recipient. Each
person in the sequence gets their own conversation: your first message to them,
and then each follow-up step as another reply in that same thread. downcroc
does not gather different people into one thread, and it does not start a fresh
conversation for a later step.

## The same mailbox carries it

The mailbox that sent the original message is the one that sends its
follow-ups. This keeps the thread coherent for the person reading it: the
reply comes from the same place the first note came from. Because of that, you
do not pick a different sending mailbox for a follow-up step. If the thread
needs to move, the move happens on the original work, not halfway through the
conversation.

Threading is mostly something downcroc handles for you. Your job is to decide
what to say and when; downcroc's job is to make sure each person hears it as
the next line in the conversation you already started.

# 1.3.2.post1

`1.3.2.post1` fixes follow-up visibility for follow-ups sent as immediate/ad-hoc steps.

## Fixes

- **Follow-up visibility:** `downcroc batch followups status`, `downcroc batch thread`, `downcroc batch list --parents`, and the follow-up outlook now surface sent child follow-up batches that were created as immediate/ad-hoc steps (not declared in the campaign snapshot or as a follow-up declaration). Previously, those follow-ups could be created, threaded, and sent successfully but parent-level views showed no step/summary for them.

## Upgrade notes

- No database migrations and no configuration changes are required.
- Restart the daemon/service after upgrading to load the updated behavior.
